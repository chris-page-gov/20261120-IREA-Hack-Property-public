# Tower Hamlets energy data access requests

Drafted on 20 November 2026. All requests below are unsent. The purpose is
to assess which verified public-facing sites merit a retrofit investigation.
Release depends on what the data holders can provide under their access and
disclosure rules.

## Requests for the non-domestic pilot

1. **DESNZ Energy Efficiency Statistics, ND-NEED.** Could you run a small
   controlled analysis or provide an approved extract for a shortlist of
   verified public-estate Unique Property Reference Numbers (UPRNs)?
   We need annual metered consumption in kWh by fuel and reporting period,
   with match quality, shared-meter and coverage flags where held. Could
   you provide bespoke analysis with disclosure-controlled outputs in
   place of releasing identifiable records? The published contact is
   `energyefficiency.stats@energysecurity.gov.uk`.
   [ND-NEED 2026 and contact](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/non-domestic-national-energy-efficiency-data-framework-nd-need-2026#contact) ·
   [DESNZ data management policy](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/standards-for-official-statistics-published-by-desnz/data-management-policy)
2. **DESNZ Subnational Consumption team.** Could you release a
   disclosure-controlled table for 2024 non-domestic electricity and gas
   in Tower Hamlets at Lower Layer Super Output Area (LSOA) level? Please
   describe half-hourly electricity coverage and provide meter counts and
   suppression flags where releasable. We can use the finest geography
   that meets your disclosure rules. The published non-domestic tables
   go down to MSOA, and the Tower Hamlets half-hourly electricity row
   covers the borough. The release lists the same DESNZ mailbox as ND-NEED.
   [Published tables](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/subnational-electricity-and-gas-consumption-summary-report-2024/subnational-electricity-and-gas-consumption-summary-report-2024--2#accompanying-tables) ·
   [Local energy evidence](tower-hamlets-estate-evidence.md#non-domestic-energy-consumption)
3. **DESNZ and Salix Finance, Public Sector Decarbonisation Scheme.**
   Could you provide delivery and monitoring or evaluation data for
   comparable public-facing community buildings? We are looking for
   installed measures, installation or completion dates, costs and any
   measured outcomes collected. Please identify the measurement period and
   flag modelled savings separately. An approved anonymised or aggregated
   comparison would also help.
   [Scheme delivery data](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/public-sector-decarbonisation-scheme-delivery-data) ·
   [Phase 1 evaluation](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/public-sector-decarbonisation-scheme-psds-evaluation-of-phase-1)

## Later domestic work

Ask DESNZ whether secure access to domestic NEED or tailored aggregate
analysis is available for the specific evaluation and eligible applicants.
Check the approval route for the Energy Efficiency Installation Checker
(EEIC) only where a per-UPRN installation check serves an approved purpose.
[Domestic NEED access guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-energy-efficiency-data-framework-need-accessing-property-level-data) ·
[Domestic grants and performance](tower-hamlets-estate-evidence.md#domestic-grants-and-performance)

## Evidence from occupiers

Ask occupiers or estate managers for site bills with the reporting period
and meter scope, current building condition or survey records, and tenure
or lease details showing which premises they occupy and who can authorise
works. Keep any records received under the agreed handling and sharing
terms. [Building identity and quantities](tower-hamlets-estate-evidence.md#building-identity-and-quantities)
