# Warwickshire legal and policy register

Record each instrument's URI, provision, point-in-time version, extent,
commencement and any transitional rule. Record policy and professional
standards in their own categories. A screening flag starts a review and
carries the observed facts and missing facts.

| Instrument or policy | Applicability question and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Energy Performance of Buildings (England and Wales) Regulations 2012, as amended[^epb] | What premises are assessed, under which scheme and on what date? For a DEC, check occupation by a public authority, frequent public visits and total useful floor area over 250 m² as well as the relevant provisions, validity and scope. For an EPC, establish the construction, sale or letting trigger and exclusions. A UPRN can refer to part of a building. A missing register hit has several possible causes. [Government DEC guide](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/display-energy-certificates-and-advisory-reports-for-public-buildings/a-guide-to-display-energy-certificates-and-advisory-reports-for-public-buildings) |
| Energy Efficiency (Private Rented Property) (England and Wales) Regulations 2015, as amended[^mees] | Is a legally covered non-domestic property let under a relevant tenancy, subject to EPC requirements and below the applicable standard? The extant landlord guidance gives EPC E with valid exemptions for covered non-domestic lettings. Check tenure, dates, certificate, exemption evidence and scope. Track EPC B policy as a prospective change with its own status. [Landlord guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/non-domestic-private-rented-property-minimum-energy-efficiency-standard-landlord-guidance) · [EPC B interim response](https://www.gov.uk/government/consultations/non-domestic-private-rented-sector-minimum-energy-efficiency-standards-epc-b-implementation/outcome/minimum-energy-efficiency-standards-mees-in-the-non-domestic-private-rented-sector-interim-response) |
| Building Regulations 2010, Schedule 1 Part L and work-specific requirements[^building-regs] | Establish the jurisdiction, proposed work, existing/new building, approval and commencement dates. Keep Approved Documents as guidance. England's 2026 circular describes changes due in March 2027 and later for higher-risk work, with transition rules. Ventilation, fire, moisture and structural effects can matter depending on the works. [England circular](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-future-homes-and-buildings-standards-building-circular-012026/the-future-homes-and-buildings-standards-building-circular-012026-letter) · [Wales approved documents](https://www.gov.wales/building-regulations-approved-documents) |
| Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990 and planning controls | Check designation, listing extent, character, planned works and planning authority. Section 7 is a separate listed building consent check. An overlap alone does not establish the consent requirement. Historic fabric, conservation-area designations and local policies need case review. [Act, section 7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/9/section/7) · [Historic England consent guidance](https://historicengland.org.uk/advice/planning/consents/lbc/) |
| Planning data powers and the proposed England standard | Existing powers under the Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023 cover planning data processing, provision and publication. The 18 November 2026 consultation concerns a specific proposed standard. Track the approved version, secondary legislation and commencement. [Planning standard assessment](planning-application-data-standard.md) |
| Building Safety Act 2022 and higher-risk regime | Check only when relevant. The building's use, regulatory height method, storeys and type of works determine the applicable route. An OS or LiDAR estimate is unsuitable as the sole basis for a statutory height determination. [Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/30) · [Existing building criteria](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/criteria-for-determining-whether-an-existing-building-is-a-higher-risk-building-during-building-work) |
| Local Government (Transparency Requirements) (England) Regulations 2015 and transparency code[^transparency] | Identify the reporting authority, holdings covered, publication requirements and relevant exclusions. The authority's transparency workbook describes a publication view. The operational estate register may have different fields and coverage. [Transparency code](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/local-government-transparency-code-2015/local-government-transparency-code-2015) |
| Climate Change Act 2008 as amended, Government Property Sustainability Strategy and WCC policy | The national target and central-government strategy supply context. Ask WCC for its applicable council policy, baseline, estate boundary, target period and approved reporting method. A benchmark or proposed target has a different status from a duty. [Climate Change Act, section 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/27/section/1) · [Government Property Sustainability Strategy](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/government-property-sustainability-strategy) |
| UK GDPR, Data Protection Act 2018, FOIA and Environmental Information Regulations | Record access purpose, data minimisation, disclosure review, source restrictions and any applicable exception with the responsible team. A UPRN can be linkable to a person's address in context. Remove certificate address data from shared views unless rights and purpose are established. Check each OS product's terms, including separate licensed NGD routes. [ICO UK GDPR guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/) · [Data Protection Act 2018](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/12) · [FOIA](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/36) · [EIR](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/3391) · [Certificate licensing](https://get-energy-performance-data.communities.gov.uk/guidance/licensing-restrictions) |

For any later project delivery, separately assess public procurement, fire
safety, asbestos, workplace safety and any funding agreement. These depend on
the contracting body and proposed work. PAS 2038, relevant British Standards,
GovS 004, IPMS and other industry standards can provide specifications,
measurement bases and operating practice. Record the adopted edition and
whether a contract requires it.

# Coverage outside Warwickshire

| Jurisdiction | Review route |
| --- | --- |
| Wales | England and Wales certificate register and applicable joint legislation. Use Welsh building control guidance and Welsh planning and heritage authorities for works. [Welsh building regulations](https://www.gov.wales/building-regulations-approved-documents) |
| Scotland | Keep Scottish EPC data and regulatory versions separate; check commencement and transitions for the 2025 regulations and 2026 amendment. Review the separate non-domestic assessment and action-plan route. [Scottish amendment](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2026/322/body/made) · [Non-domestic assessment guidance](https://www.gov.scot/publications/energy-performance-of-existing-non-domestic-buildings-information/) |
| Northern Ireland | Use the Northern Ireland certificates regime and its data routes. Check district council/building control and planning requirements separately. [Northern Ireland regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2008/170) · [NI EPC service guidance](https://www.nidirect.gov.uk/articles/energy-performance-certificates) |

A full legal catalogue depends on building use, tenure, planned works,
jurisdiction, date and delivery mechanism. Ask the relevant specialists to
review the Warwickshire rules before any automated decision or compliance
claim.
