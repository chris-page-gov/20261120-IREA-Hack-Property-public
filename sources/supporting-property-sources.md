# Candidate references

| Source | Potential use and coverage |
| --- | --- |
| Government Property Data Standard (GovS004-PDS010)[^property-standard] | Official property data definitions and reporting context |
| State of the Estate 2024/25[^state-of-estate] | Aggregate government estate, efficiency and sustainability reporting for comparison |
| Energy performance of buildings[^epb][^epb-bulk] | The National Data Library points to aggregate statistics. The separate bulk service covers Energy Performance Certificates and Display Energy Certificates in England and Wales. Bulk downloads require GOV.UK One Login |
| OS Open UPRN and the government identifier standard[^os-open-uprn][^uprn-standard] | Ordnance Survey identifiers and coordinates for Great Britain and guidance on using property identifiers. Availability of a usable UPRN in the challenge data has not been checked |
| Defra methodology for mapping the government estate[^mapping-method] | Worked example of a separate land-parcel method and its coverage limits, developed for the Government Estate Nature Plan |
| InSite service guidance[^insite] | Context for how government estate information is managed and how authorised access works |

These are research references. No additional data files were collected from
them, and no match rates or analytical results were measured.
