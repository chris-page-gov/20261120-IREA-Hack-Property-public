# Public case

The county register records a [site](site.md) at Avon Valley School and a
proposal to extend the main building, take down a modular classroom,
adapt and extend the former caretaker's house, and carry out related
landscaping, parking and other works. It records registration on
21 January 2026 and a public notice period from 5 to 26 February 2026.
The register's application type is `Reg3 Major`. The prefix in the
reference is retained as issued. The deciding authority is WCC.[^register]

See the [issued decision](decision.md) and its [numbered conditions](conditions.md).

[^register]: [WCC register entry RBC/26CC001](https://planning.warwickshire.gov.uk/swiftlg/apas/run/WPHAPPDETAIL.DisplayUrl?theApnID=RBC%2F26CC001).
