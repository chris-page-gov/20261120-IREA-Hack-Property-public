# Scope of approval

The County Planning Authority approved the Biodiversity Gain Plan for
[permission RBC/26CC001](decision.md) on **12 August 2026**. The letter says
the statutory Schedule 7A biodiversity gain condition was satisfied and
limits that finding to the Biodiversity Gain Plan. The other
[numbered conditions](conditions.md) keep their own evidence and status.
This is separate from condition 3 on implementing the landscape planting
scheme.[^bng]
