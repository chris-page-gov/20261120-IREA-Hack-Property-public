# Numbered conditions

The [issued notice](decision.md) sets out 11 numbered conditions. The notes
below paraphrase the requirements and reasons. Use the notice for the
complete wording. The numbered conditions have unverified discharge
status in this example. The [Biodiversity Gain Plan approval](biodiversity.md)
concerns a separate statutory condition.[^notice]

## Condition 1

**Start of development.** Begin the permitted works within three years of the permission.
The stated purpose is to set the statutory commencement limit under section 91.
Notice page(s) 1.[^notice]

## Condition 2

**Approved drawings and documents.** Carry out the development in line with the listed drawings and documents, including site plan P08, subject to written approvals under other conditions.
The stated purpose is to define the permitted works and secure an acceptable standard.
Notice page(s) 1–2.[^notice]

## Condition 3

**Planting and replacement.** Implement the approved planting before occupation, to the authority’s written satisfaction. Replace qualifying plant losses or damage within five years of planting.
The stated purpose is to secure green infrastructure and biodiversity gain.
Notice page(s) 2.[^notice]

## Condition 4

**Unexpected contamination.** Report any contamination immediately. When found, obtain approval of investigation, any necessary remediation and verification before occupation.
The stated purpose is to reduce risks from contamination and protect users and the environment.
Notice page(s) 3.[^notice]

## Condition 5

**Highway improvement arrangements.** Obtain written approval of arrangements for the highway improvement works before occupation.
The stated purpose is to protect safe highway access.
Notice page(s) 3.[^notice]

## Condition 6

**Travel plan.** Implement the approved travel plan in full before occupation and maintain it.
The stated purpose is to protect safe access to and from the site.
Notice page(s) 3.[^notice]

## Condition 7

**Pedestrian and cycle crossing.** Complete the crossing before occupation, following the approved drawing and highway authority specifications.
The stated purpose is to protect highway safety and access.
Notice page(s) 3.[^notice]

## Condition 8

**Car and cycle parking.** Provide the additional car and cycle parking before occupation in line with the approved masterplan.
The stated purpose is to protect highway safety and access.
Notice page(s) 3.[^notice]

## Condition 9

**Drainage design and maintenance.** Develop the site in line with the flood risk and drainage strategy and the drainage operation and maintenance plan.
The stated purpose is to secure satisfactory drainage.
Notice page(s) 4.[^notice]

## Condition 10

**Drainage verification.** Before occupation, obtain written approval of an independent engineer’s verification of the installed surface-water drainage and the existing system it joins.
The stated purpose is to verify a satisfactory drainage installation and connection.
Notice page(s) 4.[^notice]

## Condition 11

**Community sports use.** Agree community access arrangements with the authority before occupation, after consulting Sport England. Operate the facilities in line with the approved agreement.
The stated purpose is to provide safe, managed community access to sports facilities.
Notice page(s) 4.[^notice]
