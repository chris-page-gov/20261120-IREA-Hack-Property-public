# Tower Hamlets retrofit personas and user stories

These fictional, role-based personas are design hypotheses for the hackathon.
The checks describe what a person can observe in the prototype or companion.
They have not been tested with users.

| Proposed persona | User story | Observable acceptance criterion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public estates or facilities officer | As an estates officer, I want to find a public building worth investigating so I can ask for the right evidence. | I can find Gill Street Health Centre, see the workbook rating gap, and use the tracker to identify a certificate and site meter data as next checks. |
| NHS or community service lead | As a service lead, I want to verify where a service operates so I can plan investigation or works around patient and visitor access. | I can find the location warning for Aberfeldy, follow the practice's move notice, and identify that the current premises and access arrangements need confirmation with the service. Access planning takes place with the occupier. |
| Retrofit engineer or energy analyst | As an engineer, I want to establish which premises the meter, certificate and floor area describe so I can decide what to model. | I can distinguish the tribunal's second-floor occupancy from the whole-building match and identify which metering, certificate and measured-area records I need to obtain before modelling. |
| Local public health or neighbourhood analyst | As a neighbourhood analyst, I want to compare area context and see the geography, source and date of each indicator. | I can select IDACI, open an Output Area and distinguish the Census 2021 counts from the parent LSOA's 2025 deprivation index. I can describe both at their published geography without attributing either to a household. |
| Programme sponsor or data steward | As a sponsor or data steward, I want to see what evidence is missing and which access requests are pending so I can decide which investigation to support. | I can find the MSOA release limit, distinguish the borough-only half-hourly figure, and find the draft DESNZ and Salix requests with their unsent status. |
