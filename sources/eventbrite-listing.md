# Event details

AI Enablers (GDS/DSIT) is listed as the organiser. The event runs in person
from 10:00 to 16:00 GMT on Friday 20 November 2026 at The White Chapel
Building, 10 Whitechapel High Street, London E1 8QS. Places are limited to
members of the Cross-Government AI Community of Practice.[^eventbrite-listing]

# Challenge

Small multidisciplinary teams will use the
[Government Property Estate dataset](government-property-estate.md) and
other public data to explore estate performance, energy efficiency and
retrofit opportunities. The brief asks teams to identify patterns, risks and
opportunities, prioritise buildings for investigation and communicate
evidence that supports decisions.[^eventbrite-listing]

The day focuses on collaborative learning and ends with team showcases and
discussion. There is no formal judging. The organiser welcomes approaches
such as assistants, dashboards, maps, notebooks, evidence packs, prototype
applications, decision support and agentic workflows.[^eventbrite-listing]
