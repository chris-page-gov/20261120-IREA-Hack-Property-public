Material held outside the public site

Original workbook downloads, correspondence, personal identifiers and credentials are excluded from Git. The locally tested OS NGD service and its licensed feature responses are not part of the public site. Sources behind GOV.UK sign-in are linked or summarised where appropriate; their restricted extracts are not republished here.

The site’s data download is a provisional derived register. It is not an official property ownership register, a certificate bulk extract or a funded intervention list.

Reuse of repository material

Source datasets keep their own licences and required attribution. The repository has no blanket licence covering every photograph, source note and third-party extract. Check each file’s provenance and rights before redistribution. Read the licensing decisions and source register.
