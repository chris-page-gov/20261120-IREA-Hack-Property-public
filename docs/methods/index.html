1. Start with the estate workbook

The Cabinet Office Building sheet has 53,009 rows. Rows are reported estate interests. They include freeholds and leases and can include repeated building references. Treating all rows as publicly owned is an explicit working assumption for exploration.

Original downloaded workbooks are held in ignored local storage; the public site contains the reviewed derived dashboard snapshot and the full provisional candidate register.

2. Check whether floor area is comparable

The dashboard uses IPMS 3 NIA above 1 m². This leaves 10,992 usable measured-area rows. The other 42,017 are held because 35,971 show zero area, 5,145 show exactly 1 m², and 901 use another area basis. They are evidence or comparability holds, not rejected retrofit opportunities. Thirty-three E–G records sit within this held group.

4. Keep the local pilot separate

Tower Hamlets samples the same source workbook in a different way: 63 borough rows, then 62 non-domestic rows at 38 coordinate groups, all linked to LSOAs. The IMD decile 1–5 rule leaves 51 rows at 30 locations. The workbook has no D–G EPC among that group and one expired DEC whose premises scope remains unresolved. Those local identity, certificate and deprivation checks have not been applied to the national candidate register.

5. Turn prompts into fundable proposals

A public budget holder needs a named property and intervention, an accountable owner, and comparable costs and benefits before committing taxpayers’ money.

Ownership and authority

Confirm the asset, its freehold or leasehold interest, the responsible body and who may approve works.

Energy and premises

Match current EPC or DEC evidence, occupied premises, floor area and meters. Obtain measured use by fuel and period.

Intervention and value

Survey fabric and systems; identify feasible measures, capital cost, expected energy and carbon savings, uncertainty and maintenance impact.

Delivery and fairness

Check current investment, funding eligibility, disruption, procurement and the relevant nation’s deprivation measure.

National deprivation is still a gap: 2,264 of the 2,267 prompts have an ONS county or unitary area assignment and 3 do not. These broad areas do not establish small-area deprivation deciles. Do not present a national deprivation ranking yet.

Reproduce and challenge the analysis

Read the Mermaid source and count notes, the one-page A4 diagram, and the national source and method note. The repository records observed dates and snapshot checksums. The screen is a draft method, open to review.
