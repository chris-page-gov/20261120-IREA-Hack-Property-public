Non-domestic energy consumption

The DESNZ 2024 subnational release publishes non-domestic gas and electricity down to Middle Layer Super Output Area (MSOA). Its LSOA tables cover domestic meters only. A proposed map layer would show published non-domestic figures as separately labelled MSOA context, using the official stacked CSVs. Use the published geography codes, year, fuel, units and sector definition. The Tower Hamlets extract has 34 named MSOA rows for gas and 34 named MSOA rows for non-half-hourly electricity. Label any mapped electricity non-half-hourly only. Keep the single half-hourly electricity row labelled “All MSOAs” at borough level. Keep missing or suppressed values unknown. Do not allocate MSOA figures to LSOAs or attribute them to a site. This layer is pending. DESNZ 2024 release, accompanying tables · Stacked electricity data · Stacked gas data

ND-NEED 2026 gives public metered gas and electricity benchmarks by building use and size. Choose a cohort comparable to the facility and keep any benchmark distinct from the facility's consumption. A DEC uses actual energy, with scope and period to check against the facility, especially in shared premises. ND-NEED 2026 · Certificate access routes
