Tower Hamlets energy data access requests

Drafted on 20 November 2026. All requests below are unsent. The purpose is to assess which verified public-facing sites merit a retrofit investigation. Release depends on what the data holders can provide under their access and disclosure rules.

Requests for the non-domestic pilot

  1. DESNZ Energy Efficiency Statistics, ND-NEED. Could you run a small controlled analysis or provide an approved extract for a shortlist of verified public-estate Unique Property Reference Numbers (UPRNs)? We need annual metered consumption in kWh by fuel and reporting period, with match quality, shared-meter and coverage flags where held. Could you provide bespoke analysis with disclosure-controlled outputs in place of releasing identifiable records? The published contact is energyefficiency.stats@energysecurity.gov.uk. ND-NEED 2026 and contact · DESNZ data management policy
  2. DESNZ Subnational Consumption team. Could you release a disclosure-controlled table for 2024 non-domestic electricity and gas in Tower Hamlets at Lower Layer Super Output Area (LSOA) level? Please describe half-hourly electricity coverage and provide meter counts and suppression flags where releasable. We can use the finest geography that meets your disclosure rules. The published non-domestic tables go down to MSOA, and the Tower Hamlets half-hourly electricity row covers the borough. The release lists the same DESNZ mailbox as ND-NEED. Published tables · Local energy evidence
  3. DESNZ and Salix Finance, Public Sector Decarbonisation Scheme. Could you provide delivery and monitoring or evaluation data for comparable public-facing community buildings? We are looking for installed measures, installation or completion dates, costs and any measured outcomes collected. Please identify the measurement period and flag modelled savings separately. An approved anonymised or aggregated comparison would also help. Scheme delivery data · Phase 1 evaluation

Later domestic work

Ask DESNZ whether secure access to domestic NEED or tailored aggregate analysis is available for the specific evaluation and eligible applicants. Check the approval route for the Energy Efficiency Installation Checker (EEIC) only where a per-UPRN installation check serves an approved purpose. Evidence from occupiers

Ask occupiers or estate managers for site bills with the reporting period and meter scope, current building condition or survey records, and tenure or lease details showing which premises they occupy and who can authorise works. Keep any records received under the agreed handling and sharing terms. Building identity and quantities
