Tower Hamlets estate map quick guide

Use the local prototype to shortlist public-facing facilities for energy investigations, including health centres, community facilities, courts and cultural venues.

Use the public static Tower Hamlets retrofit notebook in any browser. The companion uses the open-data map and offers seven downloadable Markdown documents, personas, four walkthrough captures, the searchable 34-MSOA energy table, an ECUK extract and the tracker. OS building evidence stays in the local workflow. Gill Street walkthrough

  1. On the demo Mac, open the Tower Hamlets map in its separate Edge tab. OS Road is the default basemap. The layer control offers OpenStreetMap as an alternative. The national dashboard is a separate demonstration.
  2. Find a facility in the list or select a map marker. The map shows 62 non-domestic workbook rows at 38 locations. One marker can contain several rows at the same coordinates. Try East London Tribunal in Poplar ward and read the address scope before using the whole-building footprint.
  3. In Neighbourhood layers, switch between IMD 2025, child income deprivation (IDACI) and older people's income deprivation (IDAOPI). Decile 1 is the most deprived nationally. Fine outlines mark Census Output Areas. Hover or tap for counts or shares of residents under 5, residents aged 60 and over, and households deprived in at least two Census dimensions. Tap to see the separately labelled parent LSOA indices. Keep the indices at LSOA level and avoid drawing conclusions about any household's or facility user's health or access from these statistics.
  4. Open Gill Street Health Centre or Young V&A for examples of spatially checked address and building matches. Read the location notes on Aberfeldy Practice and Community Paediatrics before treating either as a current candidate. Check the current occupier, premises and certificate scope before comparing energy performance or commissioning an estimate. The Gill Street register check shows how to find EPC and DEC evidence beyond the workbook.

See the runbook to start or recover the preview and the question tracker for open decisions and links to evidence. See the evidence gaps and screening method for the next phase.
