Published area context

Non-domestic energy in Tower Hamlets

DESNZ recorded electricity and gas at the meter. Its published non-domestic tables cover middle-layer areas (MSOAs) and combine commercial and industrial users. Read a site’s certificate or meter records for its own consumption.

Only non-half-hourly electricity is reported by named MSOA.

The separate 2024 half-hourly row covers the borough as a whole: 1,733.90 GWh across 2,978 meters. The LSOA release covers domestic consumption.
