Tower Hamlets public estate screening and validation method

Status on 20 November 2026: draft method for validation in Tower Hamlets. The hypothesis about missing energy evidence is pending the user's verbatim confirmation. Leave its direction, outcome, comparison groups and success criteria blank until confirmed. Reproduction and evidence collection can be planned independently. The gap assessment shows what evidence each decision needs. A separate UK draft method defines investment queues and evaluation across the four nations.

  • 2. Inventory inputs and preserve identifiers

3. Reproduce the geography before adding site evidence

  1. Check that required columns are present and unique. Verify the workbook hash and record source row count. Parse finite BNG eastings and northings. Flag missing, non-numeric, out-of-range and placeholder values. Report discarded rows and retain source IDs. Preserve the raw values to audit axis order, truncation and point precision. Use workbook latitude and longitude only as a cross-check where available.
  2. Select the pinned borough and ward features by code. Confirm the full clipped edition and BNG CRS. Decode ESRI rings with their shells and holes intact. Validate and preserve the original geometry. Use a bounding-box prefilter if needed and apply polygon coverage in EPSG:27700. In the reproducibility check, covers includes points exactly on a polygon boundary. Mark any exact boundary match for review and retain its result separately. Cross-check, rather than filter by, the workbook Local Authority label. The row labelled TOWER MILLENIUM PIER falls outside the full clipped polygon. Retain it as a named exception. Keep the dwelling in the borough reconciliation and exclude it only for the non-domestic cut. Use the Poplar ward for map context, including the London Import Building point.
  3. Group the retained non-domestic rows by exact numeric BNG coordinates. Transform the distinct points with PROJ from EPSG:27700 to OGC:CRS84 (longitude, latitude). Set always_xy=True where supported and log library, PROJ and GEOS versions, transform definition, grid availability, and the CRS and precision of both inputs. Keep the BNG values for checking borderline points. Check that transformed points are plausible for Tower Hamlets and that untransformed inputs could not pass the join.
  4. Join the points to the ONS 2021 LSOA polygons with point-in-polygon. Retain each point key, candidate areas and the resolved LSOA code, method, boundary status and confidence. Inspect holes, multiparts, invalid geometries, exact edges, nearby clipped boundaries, overlaps and no-hit cases. Prefer a full-precision official geometry for ambiguous or invalid features. Log any repair and compare classifications before and after. If multiple areas cover a point or results disagree across valid methods, leave it ambiguous until reviewed. Flag mismatches between a workbook point and a verified service address.
  5. Join MHCLG 2025 File 7 to LSOA21CD. Check unique area codes, complete coverage for the retained borough LSOAs and integer deciles in 1 to 10. Keep IMD, IDACI and IDAOPI separate. Decile 1 is the most deprived nationally. Retain ranks, scores and deciles with their metric names and reporting edition. Use published LSOA statistics at LSOA level.
  6. Join Census 2021 TS007a and TS011 by OA21CD. Use ONS OA21CD → LSOA21CD nesting, keeping both identifiers at display time. Sum under 5 and age 60 and above from age bands and households deprived in at least two dimensions from the relevant categories. Divide age counts by eligible Census residents and household counts by households. Return unknown for a zero or missing denominator. Require one join per code and reconcile counts of 169 LSOAs and 913 OAs. A map point links a site to neighbourhood context. It establishes neither a catchment nor the characteristics of individual households or service users.

Preserve recorded issue and expiry fields without inferring validity.
Workbook location coverage At least one linked row with a band per exact coordinate group. Present this alongside row coverage and flag groups whose linked rows disagree.

Use a reason for each outcome and field, rather than a generic blank. A raw missing field can coexist with a verified register match for another outcome. Use missing for expected information absent from a searched source, out of scope for positive applicability evidence, unmatched when a crosswalk fails, stale for superseded or expired evidence, ambiguous for a conflict or uncertain coverage, not published or suppressed for source disclosure limitations, and awaiting for an outstanding check. Record evidence date, reviewer and follow-up. Use an explicit measured zero only when source, period and scope support it. Preserve certificate conflicts such as The White Chapel Building’s raw DEC B and operating rating 100.

Certificate lookup procedure

  1. Confirm the current service address from a dated occupier, provider or regulator source. Search the MHCLG public non-domestic register by postcode, street and town or known report reference. Record the query, direct results link, observation date, source and certificate types searched. Retrieve each candidate's individual record. Postcodes can contain unrelated addresses.
  2. Check building name, street number, postcode, unit or floor, and UPRN where available and permitted. Record a candidate, confirmed site address, or scope-verified premises match. Capture the basis, confidence, any competing matches, reviewer and evidence needed. A site-address match can cover more or less than a workbook row or occupied unit.
  3. Record certificate type and report reference, individual URL, band and score, assessment, issue, nominated and expiry dates as applicable, register status on the observation date, area with its definition, covered space, and any available DEC measurement period, fuel labels, values and units. Keep asset EPC and operational DEC distinct. Retain links and decision-relevant fields and omit assessor personal details.
  4. Record lookup outcome and certificate status separately. Use public match not found when the completed public search returns no matching record and unavailable when access or a service failure prevents a check. Use expired or superseded when evidenced and unresolved for ambiguous address, scope or status. Record a verified address match within the stated validity dates even where scope is unresolved. Confirm applicability separately. A public search outcome does not establish whether a certificate exists outside that search.
  5. Link the observation to the site and to workbook rows only at the match confidence actually established. Keep the raw workbook fields and band-coverage denominator fixed. Confirm the DEC period, supply map and measured area with estates before treating it as a verified premises baseline. For bulk repeat work, check licensing, access and publication fields first. 5. Compare coverage and test sensitivity

  • Start with counts and denominators with an observed date. Report eligible, searched, matched, verified current, out of scope, unresolved and verified baseline as appropriate. Calculate rates only for defined populations with a non-zero denominator. Show unmapped and unknown cases. Do not silently drop them after a join.
  • Reproduce descriptive band coverage for rows and show whether a coordinate has any banded row. For a verified-premises analysis deduplicate linked certificates, group shared supplies by their actual extent, and count eligible premises once per endpoint. Compare facility function, submitting organisation or estate family, area band, lease and date mix before aggregate comparisons. Record where subgroup comparisons fail the pre-agreed minimum or have little overlap. If future data justify formal intervals, preregister the unit, strata and clustering or resampling by premises or location and account for shared LSOAs and submitters as the structure warrants. Skip inferential claims where the design or clusters cannot support them.
  • Prespecify sensitivity to national decile 1 versus deciles 1 to 2, keeping IMD, IDACI and IDAOPI results distinct. Check rows versus coordinate groups versus verified premises, reused or duplicate reports, NHS versus other submissions, and facility-use mix. Repeat with disputed points excluded or separately classified, and with a validated alternative boundary vintage or full-precision source only if the geographic question warrants it. Track changes in membership and denominator. Report each run against the frozen primary definition.
  • The present frame is a small convenience snapshot. Workbook field availability, occupier or organisation mix, scope and colocation can create apparent differences. 6. Establish energy, area and emissions at the same scope

  1. Obtain at least 12 successive months by fuel from the estate team, bill-payer or landlord and prefer 24 to 36 months for assessing changing conditions or fitting a seasonal baseline. Freeze inclusive start and exclusive end dates for each period and record the read basis, meter, building scope, fuel, source unit, import, export, generation, self-consumption where measured, estimates and completeness. If using half-hourly intervals, handle time zone and daylight saving explicitly, check duplicate intervals and gaps, and aggregate from kWh intervals.
  2. Reconcile unique meter periods, bill corrections and rollovers. Where cumulative readings are used, subtract opening from closing after checking units, reset or rollover and multipliers. For gas volume use the supplier's billed kWh or document the appropriate volume, calorific-value and correction factors. Convert other fuels with recorded factors and meter basis. Sum energy across disjoint intervals and primary supplies by fuel. Reconcile downstream submeter totals against their parent and avoid summing the same supply at both levels. Keep imports, exports and on-site use separate under the chosen boundary.
  3. Record observed energy and gap coverage before considering a top-up. Prefer a corrected bill or actual meter read. For pilot exploration, represent any approved top-up separately as observed kWh + estimated uncovered kWh by fuel and period. Estimate only missing intervals using a documented applicable model, for example a validated weather and hours model using enough actual reads. Report range, share estimated and method. No unadjusted multiplication of a partial period by a calendar fraction for a seasonal fuel. Label extrapolated, shared or incomplete values. Keep them out of a ranking claiming verified full-year consumption unless separately approved criteria explicitly cover the treatment.
  4. Match the energy boundary to surveyed floor area. Record standard (including NIA, GIA or useful floor area), measured or estimated status, common and conditioned areas and floors, site and survey dates. Keep whole-building and service-space measurements distinct. Calculate annual fuel intensity as annual kWh for the verified scope divided by matched m² under the same area convention as the comparator. Keep NIA and any estimated gross area separate. Raw footprint times storeys is an estimate for a clearly defined extent, never a substitute for an occupied survey. For complex geometry review parts and heights for any indicative volume calculation.
  5. Choose a measured ND-NEED cohort, relevant DEC reference comparison where its methodology fits, or verified peer group with compatible premises use, size range, fuel mix, year, area convention, service hours and meter boundary. Keep the benchmark type, sample size and definition with the result. Record weather station and baseline period and apply justified weather adjustments for weather-sensitive loads when sufficiently supported. Review hours, occupancy, ventilation, HVAC, plant condition, IT or clinical loads and service changes. Show raw and adjusted estimates separately with method and uncertainty. Withhold comparison where no defensible peer or adjustment exists.
  6. Calculate tCO2e = Σ (activity × matched kgCO2e conversion factor) / 1000 for each reporting period, using compatible units and a pinned period- appropriate DESNZ factors file. Document reporting year, factor version, row or factor ID, source, unit, fuel, scope and conversion alignment. For a calculator using kWh, use factors in kgCO2e/kWh. Preserve direct fuel combustion and purchased electricity emissions separately where the organisation's boundary warrants Scope 1 and Scope 2. Declare the electricity accounting basis and any separate supply-chain, generation, export or transmission treatment. Avoid mixing operational inventory, lifecycle factors and carbon savings estimates. Report no combined premises emissions if material fuels or coverage are unknown. 7. Move from evidence priority to a shortlist

Tier Evidence available Permitted judgement
Discovery Workbook and public neighbourhood context, with candidate address and rating leads. Identify sites and evidence gaps for investigation.
Verified scope Current service, premises and occupier or landlord confirmed, with dated matches and certificate or meter status recorded. Prioritise a specific evidence request or survey.
Comparable baseline Meter boundaries, 12 months by fuel, scope-matched measured area, operation and comparable peer evidence checked. Quantify baseline and comparable excess where defensible.
Measure-level appraisal Survey supports feasible measures, likely costs, carbon savings and uncertainty, with service continuity, tenure and permissions considered. Apply a documented shortlist method after review and only to eligible, comparable cases.

For each measure record system or floor scope, existing condition, feasibility, design dependency, enabling works, planned period and disruption. Retain measured quantities or labelled estimates, rate source and date, capital and operating cost boundary, contingency and sensitivity range. Compare baseline with the measure scenario using the same weather, period, service and premises boundary unless the proposal explicitly changes these. Calculate avoided tCO2e as baseline minus scenario emissions with a documented factor basis. Include any significant fuel switching, interactions between measures, overlap and rebound assumptions. Keep measured post-installation outcomes distinct from design estimates, with a defined monitoring period. When records are inadequate, assign an investigation action and retain missing cost or savings. Feasibility and access gaps

Before shortlisting validate the scoring or decision rule in writing. Define how energy use, carbon opportunity, deliverability, uncertainty and the chosen equity objective each affect priority. Use declared units and weights or thresholds, publish the rationale internally and test ranking stability, ties and missing information. Keep evidence priority separate from quantified need. Missing ratings or high deprivation alone do not quantify poor site performance or carbon savings. For excluded or incomparable premises show the reason and next evidence action.

8. Save versioned outputs and confirm ownership

Save a run manifest, schema and row counts, deduplication and exclusions log, geography review, premises crosswalk, outcomes table, descriptive counts, sensitivity results and signed decision log under a run ID. For meter work save a controlled consumption, denominator and emissions ledger, including each formula, version and flags. Make changes reproducible with script or query version, arguments, environment and hashes. Keep the as-run snapshot and a reviewed publication-safe projection separately identifiable.
