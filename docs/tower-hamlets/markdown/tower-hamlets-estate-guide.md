
# Tower Hamlets estate map quick guide

Use the local prototype to shortlist public-facing facilities for energy
investigations, including health centres, community facilities, courts and
cultural venues.

Use the public static
[Tower Hamlets retrofit notebook](https://chris-page-gov.github.io/20261120-IREA-Hack-Property-public/tower-hamlets/)
in any browser. The companion uses the
open-data map and offers seven downloadable Markdown documents, personas,
four walkthrough captures, the searchable 34-MSOA energy table, an ECUK
extract and the tracker. OS building evidence stays in the local workflow.
[Gill Street walkthrough](https://chris-page-gov.github.io/20261120-IREA-Hack-Property-public/tower-hamlets/walkthrough.html)

1. On the demo Mac, open the [Tower Hamlets map](http://127.0.0.1:4175/tower-hamlets-estate-map.html)
   in its separate Edge tab. OS Road is the default basemap. The layer control
   offers OpenStreetMap as an alternative. The [national dashboard](https://chris-page-gov.github.io/20261120-IREA-Hack-Property-public/national/?view=1&tab=dashboard)
   is a separate demonstration.
2. Find a facility in the list or select a map marker. The map shows 62
   non-domestic workbook rows at 38 locations. One marker can contain several
   rows at the same coordinates. Try East London Tribunal in Poplar ward and
   read the address scope before using the whole-building footprint.
3. In **Neighbourhood layers**, switch between IMD 2025, child income
   deprivation (IDACI) and older people's income deprivation (IDAOPI). Decile
   1 is the most deprived nationally. Fine outlines mark Census Output Areas.
   Hover or tap for counts or shares of residents under 5, residents aged 60
   and over, and households deprived in at least two Census dimensions. Tap
   to see the separately labelled parent LSOA indices. Keep the indices at
   LSOA level and avoid drawing conclusions about any household's or facility
   user's health or access from these statistics.
4. Open Gill Street Health Centre or Young V&A for examples of spatially
   checked address and building matches. Read the location notes on Aberfeldy
   Practice and Community Paediatrics before treating either as a current
   candidate. Check the current occupier, premises and certificate scope before
   comparing energy performance or commissioning an estimate. The
   [Gill Street register check](https://chris-page-gov.github.io/20261120-IREA-Hack-Property-public/tower-hamlets/evidence.html#gill-street-health-centre-public-register-check) shows how to find EPC and DEC evidence beyond the workbook.

See the [runbook](https://chris-page-gov.github.io/20261120-IREA-Hack-Property-public/tower-hamlets/runbook.html) to start or recover the
preview and the [question tracker](https://chris-page-gov.github.io/20261120-IREA-Hack-Property-public/tower-hamlets/tracker.html) for open
decisions and links to evidence. See the
[evidence gaps](https://chris-page-gov.github.io/20261120-IREA-Hack-Property-public/tower-hamlets/gaps.html) and
[screening method](https://chris-page-gov.github.io/20261120-IREA-Hack-Property-public/tower-hamlets/method.html) for the next phase.
